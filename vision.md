# Company Vision

## Mission

Build an engineering company that combines experienced people with AI agents to deliver faster, smarter, and more reliable services.

Our goal is to solve real operational problems for customers while continuously improving internal efficiency through automation, standardization, and knowledge sharing.

---

## Vision (2026–2030)

Infinity Power 89 will become one of Thailand's leading AI-driven engineering service companies.

Every employee and every AI agent will work from the same company knowledge base, enabling consistent decision-making, faster execution, and higher service quality.

---

## Long-term Objectives

### 1. AI First Operations

Every repetitive process should be supported or automated by AI whenever practical.

Examples:

- Customer support
- Proposal preparation
- Preventive maintenance planning
- Reporting
- Internal documentation
- Project tracking

---

### 2. Build Company Brain

All company knowledge belongs inside Company Brain.

Knowledge should never remain only in someone's memory.

Every important decision must be documented.

---

### 3. Long-term Customer Relationships

Our business is based on trust.

Success is measured by long-term partnerships rather than one-time sales.

We aim to become our customers' permanent engineering partner.

---

### 4. Continuous Improvement

Every completed project should improve the next project.

Every mistake should become documented knowledge.

Every routine task should eventually become a reusable workflow.

---

### 5. AI Team

Build a team where humans and AI collaborate naturally.

Future AI roles include:

- CEO
- COO
- CFO
- Sales
- Marketing
- Customer Success
- Engineering
- Network Specialist
- Website Developer
- Building Automation
- Documentation Assistant

---

## Core Principles

- Solve real problems.
- Keep promises.
- Think long term.
- Document everything.
- Improve every day.
- Share knowledge.
- Use AI responsibly.
- Build systems instead of relying on memory.

---

## Success Definition

Infinity Power 89 succeeds when:

- Customers trust us.
- Employees enjoy working with us.
- AI agents help people work better.
- Company knowledge grows every day.
- The business can continue growing without depending on one person.
